# Sausage Honey Mustard Casserole

## Ingredients
- 1 tbsp vegetable oil
- 8 pork or meat-free sausages
- 1 large onion, sliced
- 2 carrots (or other root veg), roughly chopped
- 3 garlic cloves, finely chopped
- 2 tsp mixed dried herbs
- 2 × 400g cans chopped tomatoes
- 1 vegetable stock cube
- 1 tbsp honey
- 1 tbsp English mustard
- 1 × 260g bag spinach
- Salt and black pepper
- Mashed potatoes, to serve (optional)

## Instructions
1. Heat the oil in a large lidded pan over medium heat and brown the sausages.
2. Reduce heat to low–medium, add onion and carrots, and cook for 5 minutes.
3. Add garlic and herbs and cook for 1 minute.
4. Stir in tomatoes, stock cube, honey and mustard. Simmer 30–40 minutes.
5. Stir in spinach 5 minutes before serving.
6. Season and serve.
