# Boursin Pasta

![Dish Image](../Images/boursin-pasta.jpg)

## Ingredients
- 2 large onions, finely sliced (500g)
- 4 tbsp olive oil
- 150ml white wine
- 1 x 150g garlic and herb Boursin
- The juice of half a lemon juice
- 400g pasta bows
- A handful of finely chopped chives
- Salt

## Instructions
1. Preheat the oven to 180c fan. Chuck the onions into a baking dish and add the oil and a good pinch of salt. Toss
2. together and pour in the wine. Cover with some wet, scrunched up baking paper and roast for 30 minutes. Remove from
3. the oven and give the onions a good mix. Spread out, cover with the baking paper, and return to the oven for 30
4. minutes.
5. Give the onions a good mix and pop the glorious Boursin in the middle. Cover with the baking paper and return to the
6. oven for 10 minutes.
7. Meanwhile, cook the pasta for 8-10 minutes until al dente or according to packet instructions. Scoop out some of the
8. pasta water and drain.
9. Pop the onion mixture 100ml of pasta water into a blender. Add ½ the lemon and blend until creamy. Check the
10. flavour, adding the rest of the lemon and salt to taste. Pour back into the pan and add the pasta. Mix well, pouring in
11. enough pasta water to make everything go redic creamy. Most likely around 100ml. Add the chives and serve
12. immediately.
