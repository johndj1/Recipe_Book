# Spinach and Chickpea Curry

## Ingredients
- Coconut oil
- Onion
- Garlic
- Ginger
- Curry powder
- Tomato paste
- Coconut milk
- Chickpeas
- Spinach

## Instructions
1. Fry onion in coconut oil.
2. Add garlic, ginger and spices.
3. Stir in tomato paste, coconut milk and stock.
4. Add chickpeas and simmer.
5. Stir in spinach until wilted.
