# Smoky Tomato Carbonara

## Ingredients
- Spaghetti
- Eggs and egg yolks
- Parmesan
- Smoked bacon
- Tomato paste
- Cherry tomatoes
- Salt and black pepper

## Instructions
1. Cook pasta until al dente.
2. Whisk eggs, yolks and parmesan.
3. Fry bacon until crisp; add tomato paste and tomatoes.
4. Combine pasta with bacon mixture.
5. Temper egg mixture with pasta water, then stir through pasta off the heat.
