# Smoky Broccoli Pasta

## Ingredients
- Sun-dried tomatoes in oil
- Red onion
- Penne pasta
- Pork sausages
- Fennel seeds
- Purple sprouting broccoli
- Parmesan

## Instructions
1. Cook onion until soft.
2. Cook pasta and reserve water.
3. Fry sausages and add tomatoes and fennel.
4. Add broccoli and seasoning.
5. Toss with pasta and serve with parmesan.
