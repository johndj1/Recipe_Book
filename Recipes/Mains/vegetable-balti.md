# Vegetable Balti

## Ingredients
- Potatoes
- Butternut squash
- Courgettes
- Cauliflower
- French beans
- Balti curry paste
- Passata
- Vegetable stock

## Instructions
1. Parboil potatoes.
2. Steam remaining vegetables.
3. Fry curry paste.
4. Add passata and stock.
5. Stir in vegetables and simmer.
