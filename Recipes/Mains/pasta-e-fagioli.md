# Pasta e Fagioli

## Ingredients
- Olive oil
- Onion
- Garlic
- Carrots
- Celery
- Tomato passata
- Cannellini beans
- Pasta
- Vegetable stock

## Instructions
1. Sauté onion, carrot and celery in olive oil.
2. Add garlic and tomato passata.
3. Stir in beans and stock.
4. Add pasta and simmer until tender.
