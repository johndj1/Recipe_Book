# Tagliatelle with Lentil and Mushroom Ragu

## Ingredients
- Dried porcini mushrooms
- Onion, carrot, celery
- Fresh mushrooms
- Lentils
- Red wine
- Tomato purée
- Tagliatelle
- Parmesan

## Instructions
1. Soak porcini mushrooms.
2. Fry vegetables until soft.
3. Add mushrooms, lentils, wine and soaking liquid.
4. Simmer until rich.
5. Serve over cooked tagliatelle.
