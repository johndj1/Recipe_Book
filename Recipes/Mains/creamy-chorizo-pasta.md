# Creamy Chorizo Pasta

![Dish Image](../Images/creamy-chorizo-pasta.jpg)

## Ingredients
- 1 tsp olive oil
- 1 red onion, finely diced
- 10g garlic, diced
- 1 stick celery, finely diced
- 2 small carrots, finely diced
- 100g chorizo sausage, finely diced
- 500g 5% pork mince
- 1 tbsp tomato purée
- 400g chopped tomatoes
- 125ml dry red wine
- 1 tsp mixed herbs
- Salt and pepper
- 50g low-fat cream cheese
- 25g parmesan, finely grated
- 300g rigatoni
- Method
- Heat some oil in a pan, then fry off your sofrito (fancy name for finely diced onion, celery, and carrot) along with the
- chorizo. Let that spicy oil work its magic for 5 minutes.
- Stir in the garlic—just enough to make your kitchen smell like an Italian dream.
- Add pork mince and brown it like a pro. Then, in go the tomato purée, chopped tomatoes, red wine, herbs, and
- seasoning. Let it simmer for 15 minutes while you cook your rigatoni.
- If the sauce thickens too much, splash in some pasta water.
- Finish by stirring in low-fat cream cheese for that ultra-creamy texture. Serve it over the rigatoni, top with parmesan,
- and boom—you’ve got yourself a masterpiece.⁣
